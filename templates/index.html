About The Platform

What is Lung CT Imaging?

Lung Computed Tomography (CT) generates high-resolution transverse slice imagery of the thorax, mapping tissue density variations to identify tumors, infections, and interstitial lung diseases.

Why Noise Matters?

Electronic systems add Gaussian noise, while photon starvation causes Poisson shot noise. These artifacts degrade signal quality, mask fine anatomical anomalies, and compromise automated clinical diagnostic accuracy.

AI Denoising Solution

Our solution utilizes a customized U-Net++ architecture to perform pixel-level segmentation, detecting noise regions, classifying them by source, and generating quantitative severity estimations.

Deep Learning Framework: PyTorch (v2.0+)
Input Dimensions: 512 × 512 × 1 (Grayscale CT slice)
Output Classes: 3 (Clean, Gaussian, Poisson)
Classification Mode: Multi-class Semantic Segmentation

Purpose and Design

The specialized U-Net++ segmenter is designed to accurately classify scan noise and estimate pixel-level severity for CT reconstruction quality verification.

Nested dense skip pathways connect the encoder and decoder sub-networks, reducing semantic gaps between corresponding feature maps. This allows the model to detect tiny localized Gaussian sensor noise clusters and faint Poisson radiation fluctuations across the lung tissue space with minimal false positive classifications.

⚠️ Warning: Denoising algorithms should be reviewed alongside raw scans to preserve fine anatomical boundaries.
